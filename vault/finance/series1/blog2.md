# Part 2: Growth and Dividend Investing

This part covers growth and dividend investing strategies.

## Growth Investing

- Focus on companies with high growth potential.
- Often comes with higher risk.

## Dividend Investing

- Focus on companies that pay regular dividends.
- Can provide a steady income stream.
